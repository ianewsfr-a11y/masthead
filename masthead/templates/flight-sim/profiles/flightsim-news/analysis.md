# Evaluation goal

Evaluate how much a piece of flight simulation news matters to an active simulator pilot deciding what to fly, buy or update this week.

# Scoring rubric

- **9-10: Major.** A new simulator version, a major simulator update, or the release of a long-awaited study-level aircraft.
- **7-8: High value.** Release of a notable aircraft or airport, a substantial update to a popular add-on, a confirmed release date, or an important compatibility change.
- **5-6: Interesting.** Development updates with concrete details, smaller releases, previews with new information, useful hardware news.
- **3-4: Low priority.** Screenshots or teasers with no new facts, minor patches, routine sales and discounts.
- **0-2: Noise.** Off-topic items, pure promotion, giveaways, reposts.

# Evaluation guidance

Prefer shipped releases over announcements, and announcements with dates over vague plans. Consider how many pilots are affected: the simulator itself and widely used add-ons matter more than niche products. Do not reward exaggerated headlines.

Use three to five specific topic tags, such as the aircraft type, the developer, the simulator and the kind of news.
